## Slide 2: Business Metric Decomposition

* **Slide Title:** 12-Month SIP Continuation Breaks Down at the Month 2–6 "Conviction Cliff"
* **North Star Metric Equation:**
  $$\text{12-Mo SIP Retention} = \frac{\text{First-time investors with } \ge 1 \text{ successful instalment in trailing 30 days at Month 12}}{\text{Total first-time investors who started their first SIP}}$$
* **The 5-Stage Lifecycle Funnel:**
  1. *Month 0 (Setup):* Frictionless UPI setup; high initial intent and compounding optimism.
  2. *Month 1 (First Debit):* Routine verification; negligible return (+₹10); novelty active.
  3. *Months 2–6 (The Churn Cliff):* **CRITICAL DROP-OFF (40–50% churn).** First market dip occurs; dashboard flashes red returns and complex jargon (NAV, XIRR); zero contextual reassurance; cognitive fatigue triggers SIP cancellation.
  4. *Months 7–11 (Habit Formation):* Survival past Month 6 yields >75% probability of reaching Month 12.
  5. *Month 12+ (Long-Term Compounding):* Habit cemented; user begins voluntary SIP step-ups.
* **Core Takeaway:** To move the North Star, Groww must solve the psychological "Conviction Deficit" during Months 2 to 6.

## Slide 7: Picking the Solution (Champion vs. Alternatives + RICE)

* **Slide Title:** Prioritizing an In-Context AI Companion to Translate Data into Plain-Language Conviction
* **Winning Solution: 👑 GROWW PERSPECTIVE (Dynamic AI Portfolio Companion)**
  * *Steps:* User opens app ➔ AI synthesizes ledger & NAV delta ➔ Generates 3-card Monthly Debrief (Streak, RCA unit discount, Accumulator compass) ➔ In-context "Ask About My SIP" assistant answers queries with SEBI eval-gates.
  * *Features:* Rupee-Cost Averaging Translator, Consistency Streak Tracker, Plain-Language Jargon Decryptor, Smart Exit Flexibility (Skip Month / Reschedule).

## Slide 9: User Testing & Next Iteration Backlog (Part 6)

* **Slide Title:** User Testing Validates Plain-Language Reassurance While Highlighting Flexibility Needs
* **Testing Scope:** Tested live production MVP with 3 target segment users on real investment tracking tasks.
* **User Feedback Summary:**
  * **What Users Liked:**
    * *Unit Accumulation Reframing:* Users reported immediate relief seeing that a -5.8% return actually bought +6.55 extra units at a discount.
    * *Jargon Demystification:* Praised the XIRR "speed limit" metaphor for removing anxiety over extreme negative percentages.
    * *Frictionless Pause/Skip:* Unanimously preferred the 1-click "Skip Next Month" option over having to cancel their bank mandate during tight months.
  * **What Didn't Work / Constructive Feedback:**
    * Initial exit flow felt restrictive until the multi-month SIP Pause selector was added.
    * Requested advance push notifications 3–4 days before debit summarizing upcoming unit projections.
* **Next Iteration (V2 Backlog):**
  1. Pre-debit push notifications with projected unit accumulation 3 days prior.
  2. Multi-year goal bucketing (tagging funds for "Travel" or "Emergency").

## Slide 10: GTM Rollout, Metrics Framework & Risk Mitigations (Parts 7 & 8)

* **Slide Title:** Phased Rollout Plan and Metrics Framework to Defend the 12-Month Retention Goal
* **4-Phase GTM Strategy:**
  * *Phase 1 (Validation - W1–W2):* Internal dogfooding and eval-gate benchmark validation on 20-item Golden Set.
  * *Phase 2 (Beta Launch - W3–W6):* Release to 10,000 first-time investors entering Month 3 on Groww. Monitor support deflection.
  * *Phase 3 (A/B Staged Rollout - W7–W12):* 50/50 randomized control trial during next market correction. Measure Net Negative-Market Retention Delta.
  * *Phase 4 (Scale & Full Rollout - M4+):* 100% rollout across all first-time mutual fund investors on Groww.
* **Metrics Architecture:**
  * *North Star Metric:* 12-Month Active SIP Continuation Rate for First-Time Investors.
  * *Lagging Metric:* Month 2–6 Churn Cliff Survival Rate (>75% post-M6 retention).
  * *Leading Indicators:* Story Completion Rate, Flexibility Adoption (Skip/Pause vs. Cancel), AI Query Resolution Rate.
  * *Guardrails:* Zero SEBI Advice Breaches (Strictly 0%), Cancellation Friction (<15s, zero dark patterns), Arithmetic Grounding (100%).
* **Pre-Mortem Risks & Mitigations:**
  * *SEBI Regulatory Risk:* Handled via Tier 1 Advisory Interceptor and mandatory SEBI educational disclaimers.
  * *Arithmetic Hallucination Risk:* Handled by deterministic Python ledger pre-computation (LLM does zero math).
  * *App Latency Risk:* Handled by asynchronous overnight pre-computation (zero user page load latency).
