# User Research Interviews: SIP Management & Retention

This document contains primary user interview notes conducted with retail investors to understand their first-time SIP journey, ongoing portfolio monitoring, friction points, mental models, and reasons for considering pausing or stopping their SIPs.

---

## Interview 1: Jai Mohan

* **Age:** 36
* **Platform Used:** Zerodha Coin
* **When Started:** 2021
* **Total Funds Invested In:** 4
* **Active Funds:** 3 (Stopped 1 ELSS fund in 2024 due to migration to the new tax regime where 80C deductions were no longer beneficial)
* **Funds < 12 Months Old:** 1

### Detailed Responses

1. **First SIP Experience & Platform Choice:**
   * Started with an ELSS fund at ₹5,000/month on manager's advice, later stepped up to ₹10,000/month primarily for Section 80C tax returns.
   * Chose Zerodha Coin on friends' recommendations; never tried Groww. Felt Zerodha had no hidden costs, was transparent, and had a simple, uncomplicated UI.

2. **SIP Lifecycle & Payment Mechanics:**
   * Started new funds across 2023, 2024, and 2025. ELSS was cancelled/stopped in 2024 purely due to switching tax regimes.
   * **Payment Method:** Reminder arrives from Coin via Email/WhatsApp; makes payments manually.
   * **Reason for Not Using AutoPay:** Income/cash flow variability in certain months made him hesitant to enable automated debit mandates.
   * **Initial Intent vs. Current Intent:** Started strictly as a tax-saving vehicle; has now transitioned to long-term wealth creation.

3. **Dashboard & Portfolio Checking Habits:**
   * Checks infrequently—once a month or once every 15 days.
   * Looks at: percentage increase/decrease, expense ratio changes, candlestick charts, return projections, commission percentages, and exit load modifications to evaluate if holding the fund still makes sense.

4. **Challenges & Stopping Triggers:**
   * Felt an urge to pause or reduce in 2021 when first starting out.
   * Faced doubts initially regarding mechanics (e.g., smallcase concepts, money transfer flows).
   * Noticed zero guidance, tutorials, or FAQs for first-timers in smallcase.

5. **Desired Changes & Retention Enablers:**
   * Unaware if AutoPay exists within Zerodha Coin.
   * Would value automated performance glance emails comparing 6-month fund performance and tracking expense ratio changes.
   * Continuation driver: Timely payments, zero payment failure, and same-day unit allocation.

## Interview 2: Sushant

* **Age:** 27
* **Platform Used:** Groww
* **When Started:** 2025
* **Total Funds Invested In:** 3 (all started together)
* **Active Funds:** 3 (None stopped, missed, paused, or reduced; even stepped up SIP amount)
* **Funds < 12 Months Old:** 0

### Detailed Responses

1. **First SIP Experience & Intent:**
   * Started SIPs for long-term compounding and securing his future. Did independent research and committed with a 10-year investment horizon.
   * Chose Groww in 2025.

2. **SIP Lifecycle & Payment Mechanics:**
   * Enabled UPI AutoPay mandate with zero setup issues.
   * Receives notifications via SMS and email 1-2 days before debit; relies primarily on email.

3. **Dashboard & Portfolio Checking Habits:**
   * Checks the Groww dashboard randomly, prompted by market news and insights seen on Instagram (e.g., Moneycontrol).
   * Checks: Percentage changes, numerical gains/losses, return projections, unit allocations, 5-month return graphs, and cross-references against macro news.

4. **Frictions, Confusion & Out-of-App Workarounds:**
   * **Confusion:** As a complete beginner 1 year ago, experienced zero onboarding guidance and found the UI confusing.
   * **Workaround:** When uncertain, relied on Google Search and Gemini rather than in-app help.
   * **Support Experience:** Had a 10-day delay in fund allocation. Contacting Groww support was frustrating—was told to raise an in-app ticket or kept on hold until forced to disconnect. Promised callback with an advisor never materialized.

5. **Biggest Challenge & Stopping Trigger:**
   * **Direct Quote / Insight:** *"As a first-timer, identifying features, how to invest, and how to track the dashboard was very difficult. At some point, it made me consider stopping my SIP because I did not know what to track, where to track, and how to do it. Thought this would be hectic with my daily life and routine."*

6. **Desired Improvements & Retention Enablers:**
   * A dedicated navigation pane and beginner onboarding UI for first-time users.
   * Existing AI chatbot in Groww provided little to no practical help.
   * Continuation drivers: Zero autopay failures, seamless fund allocation, transparent communication regarding debits, and returns beating inflation.

## Interview 3: Bijoy

* **Age:** 38
* **Platforms Used:** Groww & smallcase
* **When Started:** 2019
* **Total Funds Invested In:** 12
* **Active Funds:** 5
* **Funds < 12 Months Old:** 0

### Detailed Responses

1. **First SIP Experience & Journey:**
   * Started in 2019 with Groww and smallcase for long-term compounded wealth creation.
   * Started with ₹100 across 3 funds, gradually expanded to 6 funds.
   * Lived through COVID crash (portfolio dropped to -₹20,000) and minor losses in 2026. Withdrew one poorly performing fund after a year of consistent losses.

2. **Payment Mechanics:**
   * UPI AutoPay enabled.
   * **Friction:** Groww sends an intimation only 1 day before debit. Requested intimation 3-4 days in advance to ensure sufficient bank balance.
   * Experienced payment deduction on smallcase where units were not allocated until support rectified it.

3. **Dashboard & Portfolio Checking Habits:**
   * Checks very frequently during volatile markets; otherwise checked once a month during normal times (checked heavily in first 2 years due to curiosity).

4. **Biggest Challenge & Handholding Deficit:**
   * **Direct Quote / Insight:** *"As a first-time investor in SIPs or any financial instruments, it was very confusing about how SIP works, what to check in dashboards, and what criteria to check before selecting a fund. I understand no one gives investment advice, but there was no handholding. Support does not help first-time investors."*
   * **Workaround:** Had to turn to Google and YouTube to make sense of the platform and terminology.

5. **Desired Changes & Retention Enablers:**
   * Groww wallet only allows investing in Stocks, not Mutual Funds (wishes MF wallet investments were supported).
   * **Key Solution Idea:** A separate UI for first-time investors stripped of overwhelming information to make the journey less intimidating.
   * Continuation driver: Market stability (preventing severe drawdowns like COVID).

## Interview 4: Pradhul

* **Age:** 36
* **Platforms Used:** Groww & Kuvera
* **When Started:** 2021
* **Initial Capital:** ₹10,000 across 3 funds
* **Active Funds:** 1 (Active since Nov 2025; withdrew 2 funds to buy a car; paused when he lost his job)
* **Funds < 12 Months Old:** 1

### Detailed Responses

1. **First SIP Experience & Journey:**
   * Learned about SIPs from YouTube; analyzed annualized returns and 5-year growth graphs before starting.
   * Invested ₹5,000/month, stepping up/down dynamically.
   * Abandoned Kuvera for Groww because Groww tracked external funds in a consolidated view.

2. **Payment Mechanics:**
   * UPI AutoPay enabled.
   * **Friction:** Like Bijoy, notes Groww intimates only 1 day prior; wants 3-4 days notice to maintain funds.

3. **Dashboard & Portfolio Checking Habits:**
   * Checked **5 to 6 times daily** when holding 3 funds. Focused on percentage increase/decrease of holdings and real-time fund returns.

4. **Biggest Challenge & Overwhelming UI:**
   * **Direct Quote / Insight:** *"Finding the right fund for investment in SIP; too much information in UI, not user-friendly for a less knowledgeable person in financial instruments."*
   * **On First-Timer Experience:** *"Since too much info in Groww was very overwhelming, if it was simplified for a first-timer it would be great. No information on metrics. Lot of numbers getting updated in real-time without clarity on what they mean. No info provided in layman language. Everything is tailor-made for veterans in investments."*

5. **Stopping Triggers & Desired Features:**
   * Considered pausing/stopping when losing a job (cash flow shock).
   * **Missing Feature (Goal Bucketing):** Desires the ability to tag/bucket SIPs by life goal (e.g., travel, buying vehicle, buying house) rather than generic fund names.
   * Continuation driver: Extreme flexibility—knowing he can pause, reduce, or stop anytime without penalty.

## Interview 5: Pradeep

* **Platform Used:** Groww
* **When Started:** January 2026 (< 12 months old)
* **Total Funds Invested In:** 2
* **Active Funds:** 2 (None paused, reduced, or stopped)
* **Funds < 12 Months Old:** 2

### Detailed Responses

1. **First SIP Experience & Investment Intent:**
   * Started in Jan 2026 on Groww with 2 funds; invested around ₹1.5L – ₹2L per month.
   * Core goal was steady long-term wealth creation. Monthly automated deductions felt disciplined.
   * Initially felt uncertain about how the money was actually working, but gained some confidence over the first few months.

2. **Ongoing Portfolio Monitoring & Market Dips:**
   * Stays consistent with long-term intent, but checks portfolio every month.
   * Admits to feeling uncomfortable and anxious when the market dips.

3. **Dashboard & Operational Friction (The "Black Hole" Delay):**
   * **The Incident:** Bank deduction happened, but mutual fund units did not show up immediately in the Groww portfolio.
   * **The Reaction:** Kept checking repeatedly, wondering if something had gone wrong. Later realized it was standard T+1 / T+2 settlement time, but at the time it felt confusing because the app failed to communicate whether it was delayed or had failed.
   * Felt the app offered inadequate transparency and explanation around unit allocation processing.

4. **Stopping Trigger / Moment of Serious Doubt:**
   * **Direct Quote / Insight:** *"The delay in allocating funds. Seriously thought my money was gone. At that point, he asked himself whether it was worth continuing."*

5. **Workaround & Lack of Support Faith:**
   * Checked online and asked investing friends.
   * Did not reach out to customer support because he assumed it was a system design limitation and felt support would not provide a faster resolution than peers.

6. **What He Checks in the App:**
   * Verifies if SIP is processed and units are allocated; refreshes the screen repeatedly while status shows "Pending" or "Processing".
   * Checks NAV once allocated.
   * Monitors overall portfolio value and ongoing fund performance.

7. **Desired Improvements & Retention Enablers:**
   * **Key Solution Idea:** A transparent, step-by-step progress tracker for first-timers (e.g., *"Money debited from bank → Order sent to AMC/Fund House → Units allotted at today's NAV"*).
   * Clear contextual explanations in the app during transaction processing to eliminate "money disappeared" panic.
